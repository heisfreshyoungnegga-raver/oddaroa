Questions, answered

The things people actually ask about Oddaroa. Tap a question to open it.

What is Oddaroa?

A football odds and predictions site. Today's matches from 11 leagues with model odds, a model pick on every match, live scores, results, league tables and a bet slip builder. Free to use, no account needed.

Are these real bookmaker odds?

No. Fixtures, scores and tables come live from ESPN, but ESPN publishes no betting odds — so every price on Oddaroa is a model odd generated for that exact match (the same match always shows the same price). Use them to compare matches and build slips; your bookmaker's price is the real one when you bet.

Can I place a bet on Oddaroa?

No. Oddaroa takes no bets and holds no money. Build your slip here, generate the booking reference on the My Slip page, then place the bet with your own licensed bookmaker if you choose to.

How does the model pick work?

For each match the model prices the main markets, then picks the single outcome it rates most likely, with a confidence score from 52% to 88%. It is a comparison tool, not a crystal ball — favourites lose every week. The Guide explains confidence in full.

How does the slip work?

Tap any odd on any page and it lands on your slip, saved in your browser. The slip drawer shows running total odds; the My Slip page adds a stake calculator, a booking reference and a copy-as-text share. One pick per market per match — tapping a different outcome in the same market replaces it.

What does VIP include, and what does it cost?

The full prediction list for every match, the best bet of the day, the accumulator of the day and VIP-only markets, delivered on WhatsApp. Weekly is GH₵50, Monthly is GH₵150. Join from the VIP page. It is the same model as the free page, just all of it — no different, magic model exists.

Do your tips always win?

No, and run from anyone who says theirs do. Football is uncertain; our free picks are published openly every day precisely so you can judge the model over time before spending anything — including before paying for VIP.

Which leagues do you cover?

Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Champions League, Europa League, MLS, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga and the Turkish Süper Lig — see the Leagues page for tables and matches.

Why does a page show fallback or featured matches?

If the live ESPN feed cannot be reached from your connection, the Matches page shows a small set of featured fixtures so the site still works. Live, Results and Upcoming pages show an honest empty message instead of invented data.

Is betting safe? What if it stops being fun?

No betting is safe — treat it as paid entertainment with money you can afford to lose, never as income. Oddaroa is 18+ only. If you are chasing losses, hiding bets or borrowing to bet, stop and get support; the About page carries our full responsible-gambling note.
