How the accas are built

Safe (4 legs) takes the four highest-confidence picks priced at 2.00 or under. Value (6 legs) takes the six highest-confidence picks regardless of price. Both rebuild themselves every day from the live fixture feed — come back tomorrow and they will be different, because the matches are different.

Combined odds multiply: four legs at 1.50, 1.70, 1.80 and 1.60 combine to about 7.34. That also multiplies the risk — a 6-leg acca where every leg is a genuine 70% shot still lands less than 12% of the time. Stake accordingly: small, fixed, and never money you need.
