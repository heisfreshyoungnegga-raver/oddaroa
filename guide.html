The betting guide

Everything on Oddaroa, explained plainly — what the numbers mean, what each market is really asking, and the money rules that matter more than any tip.

How odds work

Decimal odds are a multiplier. Stake GH₵10 at 2.50 and a win returns GH₵25 — your stake back plus GH₵15 profit. Odds also imply a probability: 2.50 implies 40% (1 ÷ 2.50), 1.50 implies about 67%, 4.00 implies 25%. Bookmakers build a margin into those implied probabilities, which is why the three outcomes of a match always add up to more than 100% — and why beating the bookmaker long-term is hard for everyone, tipsters included.

Oddaroa's prices are model odds: generated per match by our own model, the same match always gets the same price. They are for comparing matches and building slips — your bookmaker's live price is the one that counts when you actually bet.

The markets, explained

Match Result (1X2)

The simplest call: 1 = home win, X = draw, 2 = away win, over 90 minutes. Roughly a quarter of football matches end in a draw, which is why X is so often the value nobody wants to pick.

Double Chance (1X, 12, X2)

One bet covering two of the three results. 1X wins if the home team wins or draws — safer than 1 alone, so the price is shorter. This is the market for when you like a team but do not trust them.

Over / Under 2.5 Goals

A bet on the total goals in the match, by both teams together. Over 2.5 needs at least 3 goals; Under 2.5 wins with 0, 1 or 2. Watch the teams' styles, not just their names: two defensive sides can make Under the smartest boring bet in football.

Both Teams To Score (BTTS)

Yes: both teams score at least once. No clean-sheet thinking required — a leaky favourite and a brave underdog is the classic BTTS profile. One team failing to score kills it, however the rest of the game goes.

Accumulators

Several picks combined into one bet: every leg must win. Odds multiply together, and so does the chance of losing — five legs that are each 75% likely still combine to under 24%. Keep accas small, cheap and fun; our Accumulator of the Day is built exactly that way.

How to read model confidence

Every Oddaroa pick carries a confidence score from 52% to 88%. It is the model's own rating for that single pick — a way to compare today's picks against each other, nothing more. An 88% pick still loses about one time in eight across a season. Confidence is never a guarantee, and any tipster who treats it as one is selling you something.

Bankroll rules that actually matter

  • Set a budget you can lose. Betting money is entertainment money. Rent, school fees and business capital are never a bankroll.
  • Stake small and flat. 1–5% of your bankroll per bet, the same size win or lose. One bad weekend should never be able to finish you.
  • Never chase losses. Doubling stakes after a loss is how a bad day becomes a bad month. The next match does not know you lost the last one.
  • Keep records. Write down every bet and its result. A month of honest records teaches more than a year of tips — including whether a tipster (or a model) is actually beating the odds.
  • Know when to stop. If betting stops being fun, or you are hiding it, or borrowing to bet — stop, and talk to someone. In Ghana, the Gaming Commission's responsible-gaming line and your own people are a better call than one more slip.
